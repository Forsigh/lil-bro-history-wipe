Lil Bro - History Wipe

A browser extension that keeps chosen sites and words out of your browsing history.

You give it a list: a site, a site plus all its subdomains, a single page, a keyword, or a regular expression. It matches that list against your browsing history and erases the matching entries, either as you browse, when you close the browser, or at the start of your next session.

Preview first if you like. Preview reads your history, tells you what it would remove, and deletes nothing. Cookies, cache, passwords, downloads and site data are never touched, because the extension never asks for permission to reach them. Nothing leaves your computer: no account, no server, no network requests.
