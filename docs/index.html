Lil Bro - History Wipe

A browser extension that keeps chosen sites and words out of your browsing history.

You give it a list: a site, a site plus all its subdomains, a single page, a keyword, or a regular expression. It matches that list against your browsing history and erases the matching entries, either as you browse, when you close the browser, or at the start of your next session.

Preview first if you like. Preview reads your history, tells you what it would remove, and deletes nothing. Nothing leaves your computer: no account, no server, no network requests.

History is the only thing it wipes by default. Cookies, cache, download history and saved form text are a separate set of switches, off unless you turn them on, and they only ever run when you press a button (or at the close and start triggers, if you ask for that). Passwords are not on the list at all: Chrome removed that ability from extensions.
