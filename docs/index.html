Lil Bro Wipe – History Cleaner

Keeps the sites and words you choose out of your browsing history.

You give it a list: a site, a word, one page, or a pattern. It takes the matching entries out of your history while you browse, or at the start of your next session. Open the popup on any page and it tells you whether that page is on the list.

Cache, cookies, the download list and typed form text sit behind four presets, off until you switch one on. Eight themes, a PIN if you want the list hidden from other people on the same computer, and a keep list for the sites you never want touched.

What it does not do

  • No account, no analytics, no network requests of its own.
  • It never touches passwords, and there's no switch for it: the browser removed that from extensions.
  • And nothing is sold or shared, because nothing leaves your computer.
