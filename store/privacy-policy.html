Lil Bro Wipe – History Cleaner privacy policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Lil Bro Wipe – History Cleaner is a browser extension that deletes chosen entries from your browsing history. It does its work on your device and has no server of its own.

What it accesses

  • Your browsing history, through the browser's history permission. The extension reads history only to find the entries that match the rules you entered, and deletes only those entries.
  • Extension storage, which holds your rules and settings, plus an optional local log of what was wiped.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share any data. There is no analytics, no account and no third-party service.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. There is no server behind it.
  • It does not read page content, and it has no access to cookies, cache, passwords, downloads or site data.

Where your data lives

Your settings and the optional wipe log stay on your machine, in your browser's local extension storage. They are never uploaded anywhere. The log can be cleared at any time from the extension's options page, and removing the extension deletes all of it.

Your rule list is stored in the browser's synced extension storage. That is what lets a rule you add on one computer apply on your other computers. If you have browser sync switched on, your browser sends that list to the browser vendor's sync service so it can reach the devices you are signed in to. That transfer is made by the browser itself, under your browser account settings: the extension transmits nothing and never talks to a server. With sync switched off, or while you are signed out, the list stays a local file like everything else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Continued use of the extension after an update means you accept the current version.
