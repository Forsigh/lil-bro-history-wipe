# Security

The extension runs entirely on your computer. No account, no server, no analytics, and no network
requests of its own, which takes most of the usual attack surface away with it. What is left is the
list of sites and words you keep, which is exactly what someone else on your computer would want to
read.

That makes two things security issues worth reporting privately:

- a way to make it delete something it should not, or to keep something it should
- a way to read or change the rule list while the PIN is on

Use **Report a vulnerability** under the Security tab rather than a public issue, and include the
version (bottom of the popup), your browser, and what you did. I will answer within a few days.

Fixes go into the newest release. Older builds do not get patches, since the store always carries
the newest one and the code has no other distribution.
