## 2.0.5

The list can forget on its own now. It is the one place that keeps the names of what you cleaned, so you can pick how long it holds on: a week, a month, three months, until the browser closes, or forever like before. Whatever ages out is deleted for real, and the screen only shows what is still kept.

And there is a second keyboard shortcut: one press and everything hides behind the PIN again, wherever you are in the browser. The key it is on shows up in the Privacy tab, and the browser's own shortcut settings can change it.

This build carries everything from 2.0.4 as well: the PIN that hides the numbers with the list and the log, the unlock window, the Privacy tab, and the wipe that goes by age.
