The empty list and the empty log wear a small mark now, coloured by whichever of the eight themes
you are on. The artwork is two icons from Material Symbols, and their license rides along in the
package.

The switches, ticks and dots settle into place instead of snapping, and the row switches glide
with a little spring. With animations turned down in your system settings, none of it moves.

Counters and dates sit on figures of even width now, so a number going from 7 to 1,248 stops
pulling the line around it.
