# What changed

The short version of every release, newest first, three lines at most each. Dates, commits and zip
hashes live in [docs/VERSIONS.md](docs/VERSIONS.md).

## 1.4.0 - 19 September 2026

- The popup now says what happens to the page you are on: red with a dot when that page is one of the
  ones that gets cleaned, calm when it stays, from the same matcher the cleaner uses.
- The dead compact/classic popup switch came out of the page, the tests and the probe.
- The README and the landing page were rewritten shorter, and the packager learned the files it had
  been missing.

## 1.3.11 - 19 September 2026

- Buy me a coffee, a small button at the bottom of the settings page.
- A plain link, no script and no remote image, so the answer on the store's remote-code question is
  still no.
- The policy says the extension makes no network requests and names that one link out.

## 1.3.10 - 19 September 2026

- Your icon replaces the LB tile in the popup header and the settings header.
- Store screenshots re-shot in both languages, at the store's 1280x800.

## 1.3.9 - 19 September 2026

- Your new icons, all four, inside the package.
- The promo tiles are built from that icon now instead of the old drawn mark.

## 1.3.8 - 18 September 2026

- Both languages rewritten in the plain register: "Arm" is "Full wipe", and every preset line says
  what it costs you.
- Polish keeps Wł. and Wył.

## 1.3.7 - 18 September 2026

- The popup's labels translate properly. Polish showed "Scan" because the labels were read before the
  language was loaded.
- With a PIN on, adding says "Added." instead of naming the site.

## 1.3.6 - 18 September 2026

- Language row in the settings: Auto, English, Polski.
- The pages read the bundle themselves, so the setting beats the browser's own choice.

## 1.3.5 - 18 September 2026

- The popup is four things now: the state, add to filter list, wipe now, scan.
- The compact/classic switch, "more controls" and the counters came out of it.
- "Skanuj" in Polish.

## 1.3.4 - 18 September 2026

- "Preview" is "Scan" in English and "Sprawdź" in Polish, on both pages.

## 1.3.3 - 18 September 2026

- Every preset row says what it costs you, and the heaviest one is FULL and red.
- Adding to the list works while the PIN is on.

## 1.3.2 - 18 September 2026

- The list and the log came back to the first screen of the settings page.
- Every visible string rewritten so it reads like a person wrote it.

## 1.3.1 - 18 September 2026

## 1.3.0 - 18 September 2026
