Backup

Your rules live in the browser's synced storage, so they follow you to your other computers. Everything else, including the switches above, stays on this device.

Danger zone

When this is on, the whole history is erased instead of matching your rules. "Wipe now", browser start and browser close all mean the entire database; in instant mode every new visit is erased the moment it happens, and what is already in history is cleared too if the deep-scan box above is ticked. Cookies, cache, passwords, downloads and site data are never touched: the extension holds no permission for them. Your rules stay saved and apply again the moment this is switched off.
Known limits. Chrome exposes no shutdown hook, so "when I close the browser" is enforced at close when possible and at next launch otherwise. Deleting a history entry does not always clear a previously typed address from the address-bar suggestion list. Chrome keeps that in a separate store that no extension API reaches.
